# Beta User Guide

After registration, verify your email and sign in. The dashboard shows five steps: verify, upload or load sample data, analyze, visualize, and share by report/export. You can dismiss the guide without losing progress.

**Try a sample dataset** lets you explore without consuming dataset or storage quota. Open a dataset and choose a suggested question or write your own. Review **Interpreted as** and **Calculated using** before relying on the result.

Use **Yes** or **Not yet** under “Was this result useful?” to rate only your own analysis. General feedback supports a feature area, severity, affected flow, attachments, and optional safe technical context; raw dataset contents are never attached automatically.

DataPilot is beta software. Avoid sensitive or regulated data unless approved and independently review important decisions.
